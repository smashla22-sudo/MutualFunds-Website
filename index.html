FAQ

Common questions

Don't see your question? Send it to me and I'll reply within one working day.

What is the minimum amount to start a SIP?

Most schemes accept SIPs from ₹500 a month, and some from ₹100. Lumpsum investments usually start at ₹1,000 to ₹5,000 depending on the scheme.

Do I pay a fee for your services?

No. Consultations and ongoing service are free to you. As a mutual fund distributor I invest your money in the regular plans of schemes, and I receive a commission from the fund house. This commission is included in the scheme's expense ratio. Details are on the disclosures page.

What documents do I need to start?

Your PAN card, Aadhaar, a cancelled cheque or bank statement, and a photograph. If your KYC is already complete with a KRA, onboarding usually takes a few minutes.

Can I stop or pause my SIP?

Yes. SIPs have no lock-in (except ELSS, where each instalment is locked for 3 years). You can pause, change the amount, or stop at any time. Some schemes charge an exit load if you redeem within a set period, often one year.

Is my money safe with you?

Your money goes directly from your bank account to the fund house, and units are held in your name. I never handle your money. All mutual funds are regulated by SEBI. Note that mutual fund values do go up and down with markets.

How are mutual fund gains taxed?

Tax depends on the type of fund and how long you hold it. Equity funds held over a year are taxed as long-term capital gains above an annual exemption limit. Tax rules change, so I'll give you a capital-gains statement each year and recommend you confirm with your tax adviser.

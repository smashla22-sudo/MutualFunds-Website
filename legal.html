Nature of service

We act as a mutual fund distributor. We are not registered with SEBI as an Investment Adviser or Research Analyst. Any guidance we provide is incidental to the distribution of mutual fund products. Investors should make independent decisions and may consult a SEBI-registered investment adviser for advice.

Commissions

Investments made through us are in the regular plans of mutual fund schemes. We receive commission (trail commission) from the asset management companies whose schemes we distribute. This is included in the scheme's total expense ratio, which is higher for regular plans than for direct plans of the same scheme. Investors may invest in direct plans without a distributor. The commission received across competing schemes is available on request, and is also shown in your Consolidated Account Statement (CAS).
